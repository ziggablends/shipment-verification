1Purpose of this Request

A further payment of USD 500, described as a refundable insurance charge, has been requested in connection with the shipment referenced above. Before any additional payment is considered, the recipient of this document is asked to provide independently verifiable documentation establishing the identity of the carrier, the existence and status of the shipment, and the legal and contractual basis for the requested charge.

1.1 Background as understood by the requesting party

  1. An order was placed with a seller operating on Telegram under the username @annievendor, associated with a channel named “GBL Peptides Supplier”.
  2. Approximately USD 483 was paid in Bitcoin for the order, inclusive of shipping.
  3. After the payment was accepted, the requesting party was informed that orders shipped from a Canadian warehouse were subject to a minimum order value of approximately USD 800. An additional payment was made to satisfy this requirement, which had not been disclosed before the original payment.
  4. An entity identifying itself as “United Ace Cargo” subsequently contacted the requesting party via WhatsApp, using contact details provided during the ordering process, and requested a payment of USD 500 described as insurance, stated to be refundable after delivery.
  5. This insurance charge was not disclosed before payment was made to the seller.
  6. The explanations received regarding the shipment’s origin, warehouse requirements, and insurance obligations have differed between communications.
  7. The website presented for the cargo entity (https://united.acecargo-ex.com) appears to reference more than one business identity.

The points above reflect the requesting party’s records and understanding. If any point is inaccurate, please identify it and provide supporting documentation.
